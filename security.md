## ⚙️ Tuning Order And Parameters

The parameter table, and the order to walk through it.

| **Parameter** | **Range** | **Start at** | **Why** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target bone | chest / neck / head | chest | lowest profile |
| FOV | 4–180° | 12° | smaller is quieter |
| Smoothing | 1–100 | 52 | appearance |
| Reaction delay | 0–400 ms | 120 ms | instant reads as machine |
| Visibility gate | on / off | on | stops aiming through walls |
| Trigger assist | off / tap / hold | off | separate module |

**Walk it in this order**

1. **Bone first.** Chest. Leave it for several sessions before considering anything
   else — it is the single biggest change to how kills look.
2. **Reaction delay second.** A crosshair that acquires in zero milliseconds is not a
   human response time. 120 ms is roughly the fast end of human.
3. **FOV third.** Start at 12° and only widen when you are genuinely missing shots.
   Most people widen this far too early and never narrow it again.
4. **Smoothing last.** This is fine tuning, and only ever downward.

**Two presets that ship**

| **Preset** | FOV | Smooth | Bone | Delay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legit | 10° | 60 | chest | 150 ms |
| Standard | 16° | 45 | chest | 110 ms |

**What to leave alone**

The visibility gate. Turning it off lets the aim lock through geometry, which is the
one behaviour that looks unmistakably wrong in a replay or a spectator view.
